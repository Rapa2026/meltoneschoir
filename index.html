WHO WE ARE
The Meltones’ origins were at Sir John Deane’s College Adult Education Department back in 2008. It was Mel’s first choir that she led, so it holds a special place in her heart. There are still some of the original members singing all these years later.
MUSIC WE SING
We are first and foremost about the joy of singing. However, we strive for a good standard and sing in three-part harmony, Musical Theatre, Pop and a little bit of Classical too! If you are interested in joining us or coming along for a taster session, please click below and we will be in contact.
